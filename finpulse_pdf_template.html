WINNING BLUEPRINT

Core Brief Challenge: "A generation gets its financial education from TikTok. Build something better. Build the app that makes young people confidently financially capable — across financial health, saving, investing, crypto and AI. The one the banks should have built."

🎯 1. The 40/40 Scorecard Strategy

Cluster Target Score Winning Execution
1. Idea & Insight 10 / 10 Pitch built around persona Maya (21), confused by emergency tax on her first payslip while targeted by 100x leverage trading ads on TikTok/IG.
2. Product & Execution 10 / 10 Live interactive web app featuring multi-platform BS Scanner (TikTok, IG, YT, X), UK Payslip Decoder, and 5-Year Compound Risk Sandbox.
3. Business & Reach 10 / 10 B2B2C Model: Free for young users. Funded by UK Banks via FCA Consumer Duty compliance budgets and Employers.
4. Delivery & Pitch 10 / 10 90-second live working demo + Y-Combinator style 5-minute pitch with user iteration evidence.

🚀 2. Product Features & Modules

🛡️ Multi-Platform BS Scanner

Paste viral claims from TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or X. AI rates scam risk (0-100%), flags affiliate profit traps, and shows plain-truth math.

🧾 UK Payslip & Salary Decoder

Demystifies PAYE income tax, National Insurance, tax codes (1257L, BR), student loans, and highlights free employer pension match money.

📈 "Investors Not Gamblers" Sandbox

5-year compound growth simulator comparing diversified index ETFs vs high-leverage meme coins, featuring a Market Crash stress test.

🏦 FCA Consumer Duty Bank Portal

Demonstrates how partner banks (NatWest, Barclays, Monzo) view audit-verified customer financial literacy metrics to satisfy FCA regulations.

💼 3. Commercial & Business Model (Who Pays?)

The Payers vs. Users Alignment:
1. UK Banks & Building Societies pay SaaS licensing fees out of mandatory FCA Consumer Duty education budgets.
2. Employers & Corporates pay as a Gen Z employee financial wellbeing perk to reduce workplace money stress.
3. Parents (under 18s) pay a modest family subscription for youth financial capability building.

  1. 0:00 - 0:45 | The Persona (Maya, 21): Bring a real person into the room suffering payslip tax confusion & TikTok financial hype.

  4. 3:15 - 4:15 | Business Model: Explain FCA Consumer Duty bank funding.
